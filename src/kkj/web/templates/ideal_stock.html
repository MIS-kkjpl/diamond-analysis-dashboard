How an ideal is set, per group and branch

  1. Forecast: the branch's recent selling rate (last {{ rules.fc_level_months }} months, recent months counting more), leaning on the product and item group where the group sells rarely{% if rules.fc_trend %}, scaled by the product's trend (capped at ±{{ rules.fc_trend_cap_pct | num }}%){% endif %}.{% if rules.fc_exclude_orders %} Customer-order sales are left out.{% endif %}
  2. Demand cover: enough to meet demand for {{ rules.replacement_days }} days (replacement) + {{ rules.review_days }} (review), {{ rules.service_a_pct | num }}% of the time for grade A groups, {{ rules.service_b_pct | num }}% for B, {{ rules.service_c_pct | num }}% for C.
  3. Choice: at least {{ rules.choice_months | num }} months of forecast demand: customers choose among designs, and branches holding more of a group sell more of it.
  4. Display minimum: {{ rules.display_min_pcs }} piece{{ "" if rules.display_min_pcs == 1 else "s" }} of every group the branch sold in the last {{ rules.display_min_months }} months.
  5. The ideal is the largest of these, unless fixed by hand. The company ideal is the sum of its branches'; stock elsewhere (Head Office, wholesale) counts as held. Within ±{{ rules.ideal_band_pct | num }}% of the ideal (and at least {{ rules.turn_min_change_pcs }} units) it is left as it is.

Error = total miss ÷ pieces sold (lower is better). At each group and branch, the finest level, the error is {{ "%.0f%%" | format(100 * fine_err) if fine_err is not none else "–" }} against {{ "%.0f%%" | format(100 * fine_avg) if fine_avg is not none else "–" }} for the 12-month average: single groups sell 0, 1 or 2 pieces in two months, so much of that is chance, and the safety margin is sized from it.
