Words used in Stockwise

Group
Pieces that sell as the same thing: same purity, item and weight range, e.g. 18KT · D RING · 0.25–0.5ct. Stock is planned per group, not per design.
Item group
The broad family: Diamond Jewellery, Gold, Loose Diamond…
Ideal stock
How many pieces of a group a branch should hold: enough to keep selling until new stock arrives, enough choice on the counter, and at least one on display.
Suggested / Your ideal
Suggested is what Stockwise works out; Your ideal is the number in use. They are the same until someone types their own.
Buy more · Too much · Stop stocking · About right
What to do with a group: we hold less than the ideal, more than the ideal, there are no sales expected, or it's close enough.
Stock turn
How many times a year the stock sells through. 1× means a year's sales equal what we hold; higher is faster.
Markdown
A price reduction to help a slow piece sell.
Clearance
Selling off or melting very old pieces to free up money.
Stock transfer
Moving pieces to a branch where they sell better.
Replenishment
Restocking something that is running out.
Approval
Pieces out with a customer to try at home, still counted as our stock.
Set
Pieces labelled separately but sold together (e.g. necklace and earrings); counted once.
₹ figures
Selling value before GST.
