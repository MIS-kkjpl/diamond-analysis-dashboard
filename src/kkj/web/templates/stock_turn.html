How a brick is judged

  1. Over the last 3, 6 and 12 months, stock is read once a fortnight (6, 13 and 26 fortnights). A window counts when the brick was in stock in at least {{ rules.turn_availability_pct | num }}% of the fortnights that have stock data.
  2. Turn = pieces sold in the window, annualised ÷ average pieces held (out-of-stock fortnights count as 0).
  3. Blend: all three count → {{ rules.turn_w3_all | num }}/{{ rules.turn_w6_all | num }}/{{ rules.turn_w12_all | num }}; 6 and 12 only → {{ rules.turn_w6_of_6_12 | num }}/{{ (100 - rules.turn_w6_of_6_12) | num }}; 3 and 6 only (new) → {{ rules.turn_w3_of_3_6 | num }}/{{ (100 - rules.turn_w3_of_3_6) | num }}; 3 only → too new.
  4. Compared with the ideal turn: inside the band → keep; below → reduce; above → increase. Ideal stock = blended yearly sales ÷ ideal turn.
  5. Too little availability to judge and out of range → if the next lighter and heavier weight ranges are healthy, hold for {{ rules.turn_hold_months }} months; otherwise phase out (or, if it sells whenever stocked, stock it properly).
Turns are a year's sales rate from each window; the 3-month figure is seasonal (October is about twice a normal month).
